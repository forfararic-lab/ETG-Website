Contingent & Retained Search

One Seat. The Right Person.

Contingent when you need flexibility. Retained when the search deserves full focus. We'll tell you which one the role calls for.

This is the core of what we do: operations leadership, plant management, and technical maintenance and service roles. Most of the candidates we bring forward are veterans. Some aren't.

Transitioning service members are one part of the veteran talent pool. We also reach the people who left the service years ago and have since built civilian careers, proven in both settings and rarely looking at a job board.

Contingent Search

02
